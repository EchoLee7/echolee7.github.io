## Overview

This ongoing research project, supervised by Professor Jonathan Washington at Swarthmore College, explores hybrid approaches to Turkish–English machine translation.

The project compares rule-based, neural, and hybrid machine translation systems, with particular attention to Turkish morphological complexity.

My current work includes investigating morphological analyzer coverage, diagnosing unknown word forms, and exploring neural language modeling approaches.

**Status:** Ongoing research
