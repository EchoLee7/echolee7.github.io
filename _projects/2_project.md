## Overview

This ongoing research examines the reliability of large language models in identifying and interpreting forward-looking statements in financial disclosures.

The project focuses on how linguistic nuances, including modality and expressions of uncertainty, affect the consistency and accuracy of language model classifications.

**Status:** Ongoing research
