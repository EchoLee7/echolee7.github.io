Hi! I'm **Yike (Echo) Li**, an undergraduate at [Swarthmore College](https://www.swarthmore.edu/) studying **Mathematics and Linguistics**.

My interests lie at the intersection of **Natural Language Processing (NLP), Machine Learning, and Linguistics**, particularly how computational models learn, represent, and reason about language.

I'm currently involved in three research projects:

- **Generalizing Boundary** — Investigating how large language models discover generalizable patterns, represent exceptions, and translate natural language descriptions into formal planning representations.

- **PILOT / FORECAST** — Studying the reliability of language models in identifying and interpreting forward-looking statements in financial disclosures, with an emphasis on linguistic nuance and classification consistency.

- **Turkish–English Hybrid Machine Translation** — Exploring how rule-based linguistic knowledge and neural methods can complement each other in machine translation, with a focus on Turkish morphology and low-resource learning.

More broadly, I'm interested in developing language technologies that combine **linguistic insight with computational approaches**.
