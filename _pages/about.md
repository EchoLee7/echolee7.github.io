Hi! I'm **Yike (Echo) Li**, an undergraduate student at
Swarthmore College studying Mathematics and Linguistics.

I'm interested in **Natural Language Processing (NLP)**,
**Machine Learning**, and the intersection of language
and computation.

My current research explores Turkish morphology and
Turkish–English hybrid machine translation, particularly
how rule-based linguistic systems and neural approaches
can complement each other.

I'm excited about building language technologies that
combine computational methods with linguistic insight.
